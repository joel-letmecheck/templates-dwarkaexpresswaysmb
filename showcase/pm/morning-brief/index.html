How it works

  1. It connects to your toolsEmail, calendar and your ticket tool (such as Jira or Linear), using accounts you already have.
  2. It runs on a scheduleFor example every weekday at 8:30 AM, so the brief is waiting when you sit down.
  3. It drafts, you decideSuggested replies are drafts only. You edit them and press send yourself.

What you’d need: your work email and calendar, and read access to your ticket tool. A short setup to choose what the brief should cover.
