How it works

  1. You share the notesPaste them in, or point it at where your meeting notes or transcript are already saved.
  2. It finds the actionsIt lists each action with an owner and due date, and flags anything it had to guess.
  3. It drafts the follow-upA client status email is prepared in your tone. You edit it and press send yourself.

What you’d need: your meeting notes (typed, pasted or from a transcript) and, optionally, access to your email to save the draft there.
